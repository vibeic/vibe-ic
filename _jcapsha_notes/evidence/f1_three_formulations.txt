F1 TOOK THREE FORMULATIONS. The first two were swept and dropped. Recorded in
order, because the two that failed are the reason the third is shaped as it is.

FORMULATION 1 -- "pin the INPUT SET of a re-implementation against upstream",
    caught by a set difference.
    REFUTED. Population 5 of 1232. Natural scoping: 43 unaccounted, fires 4/5.
    Narrowed by upstream's own pdk=True flag: 19, fires 3/5, and the control on
    the pre-fix tree returns [] -- it does not catch the defect it was written
    for. Third scoping, the upstream step's own config_vars introspected from
    the running library: the variable is not in the list either.
    Structural reason: the dropped variable is declared in the PDK variable
    table and consumed in a shared helper, attached to no step.
    Full detail: f1_input_set_pin_REFUTED.txt

FORMULATION 2 -- "a refusal on absence must have READ EVERY VIEW the
    distribution ships".
    DROPPED, and I dropped it after adopting it, which is the mistake worth
    recording. It fires on the defect, and its two arms looked clean on the
    fixture -- but it was never swept. Sweeping it: population 14, of which 10
    refuse after a lookup into a distribution view, and EIGHT OF THE TEN read
    exactly ONE view. A rule that fires on 8 of 10 correct programs is the same
    bug as formulation 1, arrived at from the other side.
    Measured: f1_formulation2_sweep_MEASURED.txt,
              f1_formulation2_inscope_MEASURED.txt

FORMULATION 3 -- "a refusal that a declared name is ABSENT must be FALSIFIED
    against the views that were not read".
    It fires only when the refused NAME is actually FINDABLE in a view the step
    did not open. That is a grep over directories that exist, for a string the
    step itself chose, so there is no judgement in it -- and it CANNOT fire on
    a step that read one view and was right, because the name is not there.

    MEASURED, both arms, with the negative control that formulation 2 never had
    (`f1_final_probe.py`):

      PDK declares the name in the other view   refused 'io_site'
                                                unread ['libs.tech']
                                                findable in libs.tech/.../config.tcl
                                                -> FIRES

      PDK declares it NOWHERE (the control)     refused 'io_site'
                                                unread []
                                                findable none
                                                -> silent

    The second arm is the one that matters: a genuine NOT_FOUND stays a genuine
    NOT_FOUND. The rule refuses the REFUSAL, never the design.

WHAT THE SEQUENCE COSTS, STATED. Three formulations, two of them published in
this branch before being swept. The lesson is not about pads and not about
views: A RULE IS NOT A RULE UNTIL IT HAS BEEN RUN OVER ITS OWN POPULATION.
Both failures were invisible from the one case that motivated them, and both
took one sweep to expose.
