# Employer Consent Template (optional, one page)

> **Not legal advice.** This is a lightweight convenience template in
> the DCO spirit. Adapt it with your own counsel. Vibe-IC does NOT
> require this document — the mandatory mechanism is the per-commit
> `Signed-off-by` (DCO 1.1) plus Apache-2.0 §3/§5; this template merely
> helps corporate contributors document employer alignment.

---

## Employer Consent to Open-Source Contribution — Vibe-IC

**Employee:** ____________________ (name / email used in `Signed-off-by`)

**Employer:** ____________________ (legal entity)

**Project:** Vibe-IC (https://github.com/vibeic/vibe-ic), licensed
under the Apache License 2.0.

The Employer confirms that:

1. The Employee is authorized to contribute to the Project, and such
   contributions are licensed to the Project and its users under the
   Apache License 2.0 (including its §3 patent license).
2. The Employer will not assert, against the Project or its users, any
   patent claim that reads on the Employee's contributions as
   integrated into the Project (scope: the contributions themselves,
   per the Project's CONTRIBUTING.md patent non-assertion pledge).
3. Where the Employee's contributions were created using Employer
   resources or within the scope of employment, the Employer either
   (a) owns and hereby licenses them as above, or (b) confirms the
   Employee holds the necessary rights.

Confidential information of the Employer must not be contributed; the
Employee remains responsible for screening contributions accordingly.

繁體中文摘要（以英文本為準）：雇主確認——(1) 員工獲授權以
Apache-2.0（含 §3 專利授權）向本專案貢獻；(2) 雇主不就該等貢獻（以其
整合進專案的範圍為限）對專案或其使用者主張專利；(3) 若貢獻使用雇主資
源或屬職務範圍，雇主擁有並照上述授權，或確認員工持有必要權利。雇主機
密資訊不得進入貢獻。本範本非法律意見、非專案強制要求（強制機制為逐
commit 的 DCO `Signed-off-by`）。
