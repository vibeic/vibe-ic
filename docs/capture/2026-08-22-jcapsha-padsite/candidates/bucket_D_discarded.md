# Bucket D — discarded (1 entries)

- **capture-lane**: REFUTED BY MEASUREMENT, and recorded so it is not filed again. The prior lane observed that sweeping the vertical rotation argument left the east and west pads unchanged, and concluded the argument was inert; a row-level dump then showed the horizontal arguments feeding the east and west rows and the vertical arguments feeding the north and south rows, which reads as a transposition and was drafted here as a forked-tool defect. It is not one. The tool's own option table documents the horizontal argument as naming the site for the horizontal pads, east and west, and the vertical argument as naming the site for the vertical pads, north and south, and it further documents the vertical rotation default as belonging to the southern row. Every number measured is the documented behaviour of a tool that is self-consistent with its own contract. There is no defect to fix in the fork, and a fork-side change here would break every caller that reads the documentation. The defect is entirely on our side, and it is that our step bound the word vertical to the vertical SIDE while the tool binds it to the vertically-oriented PAD. That is not a discard: it is captured as the upstream-pinning rule and as the false-reason defect in this same batch.
