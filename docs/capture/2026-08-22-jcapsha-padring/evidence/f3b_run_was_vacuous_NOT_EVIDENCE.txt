A RUN THAT EXAMINED NOTHING CANNOT CONFIRM WHAT I WANTED IT TO CONFIRM.

Having written "a finding is not a finding until the thing it describes has been
RUN", I applied it to my own F3b claim -- "0 of 4 instances carry the
non-honouring as a machine-readable record" -- which was a GREP result, the exact
shape that had just failed me on F3c.

So I ran one of the four:

    rc 0
    VACUOUS_PASS: no analog_block_list.json under <empty dir> -- nothing was
    examined; this is not a finding that the design is digital-only

    mentions of the construct it documents as un-honoured, in its output: 0

AND THAT ZERO IS WORTH NOTHING. The program examined no blocks, so it had no
occasion to disclose anything about them. A vacuous run's silence is not evidence
of silence on a real run -- it is the absence of a run. Counting it would be the
wrong-shaped verification: it would let me retire the question with the
authority of having "checked", which is worse than leaving the question open,
because nobody re-opens a box marked checked.

(The program's own VACUOUS_PASS line is, separately, exactly the disclosure
behaviour this repo's denominator rule asks for. It said plainly that it
examined nothing. That is why I could tell.)

STATUS OF THE F3b SUB-CLAIM: the "0 of 4" is a SOURCE-level result and is
labelled as one. Confirming it at RUNTIME needs a real project fixture per
instance -- an analog block list with a hardmacro for this one -- which this
capture does not build. Left open and named, rather than closed on a vacuous
run.
