M3 IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS. The first test's docstring says "the arithmetic
closing is necessary and NOT SUFFICIENT -- two wrong numbers can still sum
correctly". That was an assertion when written. M3 is the demonstration: a pair
of wrong numbers that sums to 20, sails past the arithmetic check, and is caught
only by the measurement against upstream. The two tests are non-redundant, and
this is the evidence rather than the claim.

M2 is worth reading too: ONE failure is the CORRECT outcome, not a gap. A test
that fired there would be claiming something false.
