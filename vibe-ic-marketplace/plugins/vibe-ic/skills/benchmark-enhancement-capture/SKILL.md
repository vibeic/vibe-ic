# Capture a proven recovery

Read `CHARTER.md`. Capture a recovery when AI review finds and resolves a real
problem in a native result. The purpose is to make the responsible tool,
producer, or expert craft better on the next applicable input.

## Establish the recovery

1. Preserve the visible input, native tool command, original limit, observed failure, and actual corrected result outside the plugin repository.
2. Explain the cause in terms of the failed requirement and its actual consumer.
3. Distinguish a design error, a local tool defect, and a plugin defect. Fix the responsible one.
4. Verify the fix through the native tool and relevant existing tests without moving a limit.
5. State where the fix generalizes and what remains unmeasured.

For benchmarks, use `benchmark_dispatch.py` for Program First, independent
blind AI review and backup, and official scoring. Authors and reviewers read
only the provided prompt and context. They do not read hidden oracle,
harness, or golden contents. A rejection requires a prompt-derived executable
challenge, and a repair must satisfy that challenge and the normal product
verification before a fresh independent review.

## Put the fix with its owner

Consult `benchmark/CAPTURE_ROUTING.json` for the responsible program and skill.
For native flow glue, use the actual caller of the tool in `flow_engine.py`,
`flow_native_adapters.py`, or the relevant native helper. For native tool
behavior, fix the local tool fork. For document extraction or RTL generation,
fix its existing producer. Do not add a checker merely to repeat another
checker or to satisfy an obsolete wrapper.

A reusable technical lesson belongs in the applicable expert skill or IC
Expert knowledge, with the same input-only boundary as the original work.
Keep the lesson general. Do not copy a design's identity, customer data,
source text, hidden expected answer, or source-specific values into general
control logic. Preserve the IC Expert DB and per-layer craft.

Capture the reproducible discrepancy, affected source fact or native result, owning design/tool/plugin fix and the verification performed. Keep this evidence outside the plugin repository when required by the task.

## Verify and deliver

Run the next owner-requested verification on the changed product path. Keep
any owner-requested cohort size or repetition count. Preserve independent
blind actors and the official scorer, and disclose substitutions or missing
measurements. Do not claim a speedup, a benchmark score, or whole-design
acceptance from metadata alone.

Keep run results separate from product code. Use the current task's
authorization for changes, external reports, messages, PRs, pushes, and landing.
The owner's known landing workflow is `~/claude-leader/land_one.sh`; retain
`gen_program_inventory.py`, `nda_diff_scan_check.py`, and
`tools/release/assign_version.py` and use their actual instructions. This skill
does not add another release policy or authorize a landing on its own.
